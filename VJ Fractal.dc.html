Settings — saved on this machine
Res, Fps, layout, palette and its Sat / Gam / Span / Warm trim, Smth, FX, Spin, LFO division, crossfade type and length, quantise, gyro, mesh source and Auto all come back the way you left them. So do your slots, cue list and scene messages. Live performance state — the current scene, pan and zoom, camera, strobe kill — deliberately does not, so the app never reopens mid-set.
Accent
Colour of every lit control, meter and readout in the interface. Saved with the rest of the settings.
Pad map — every button has one job
1 L2 — Zoom out
2 L1 — Modifier — hold it
3 R2 — Zoom in
4 R1 — Second modifier — hold it
5 D-pad ← → — Previous / next scene
6 D-pad ↑ ↓ — Step the colour palette — 14 of them, either direction
7 Left stick — Orbit (3D) or travel across the frame (2D). Rides the surfer on Shorebreak. Press for auto-spin on-off
8 Right stick — Morph and Detail. Press to recentre the view
9 Share — This help panel
10 Options — Cycle every automation source
11 Touchpad — Hold the click and use both sticks for fine constants
12 △ Triangle — Show or hide the stored message
13 ○ Circle — Show or hide the controls
14 ✕ Cross — Automation on / off
15 □ Square — Randomize the parameters
16 PS button — Strobe kill
Hold L1 with the face buttons: □ layout, △ next stored message, ✕ crossfade, ○ FX chain.
